Where to stay

Change hotels at most once

D1–D5: Songshan Lake / Dalang

Prioritise reliable course commuting. Search listings place Aloft, Hampton, Hyatt, Ramada, Ascott / Citadines and other levels roughly one to four kilometres from DiveHub, but verify the actual entrance and peak drive time.

Filter by: real drive time, free cancellation, laundry / wetsuit drying, quiet air-conditioned room, early breakfast, nearby light food and easy ride-hailing. Do not sacrifice course logistics for a lake view.

D6: Guancheng / Nancheng

One move can make Keyuan, Liyuzhou and Humen easier. Staying in Songshan Lake is also valid if extra ride-hailing cost is acceptable.

Price strategy

Without dates, do not publish a “lowest current price”. Hold ¥200–450 per night for a cancellable room. Weekend, exhibition and holiday rates can rise sharply. A five-star lake hotel is a recovery-day upgrade, not the baseline.

Ask before booking

  • Where wet swimwear / wetsuits may dry;
  • Self-service laundry and drying;
  • Breakfast time and takeaway option;
  • Deposit, parking, invoice and cancellation terms;
  • Night access and ride-hailing pickup;
  • Pool rules. Even if fins are allowed, never practise breath-hold alone.

For a surf add-on, book one separate coastal night within walking distance of the instructor meeting point, with cancellation and weather-closure terms.
