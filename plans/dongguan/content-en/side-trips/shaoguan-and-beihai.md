## A: Shaoguan Danxia Mountain + Guangdong Grand Canyon

This is materially different from Guilin and Yangshuo: red sandstone peaks, cliffs and gorges rather than limestone karst. It also keeps the return to Changsha relatively direct. Do not try to clear every area in one day.

### 24 August: move after the course

- 10:00–13:00 is only a planning example for the course and assessment, not a guaranteed release time;
- 13:00–15:30: eat, shower, rest and confirm any make-up work or extra night;
- After 15:30, travel from Songshan Lake to Humen station and take an evening train to Shaoguan;
- Stay near Shaoguan station or outside Danxia Mountain;
- Do not force sightseeing on the course-transfer afternoon or compress recovery to catch a train.

### 25 August: Danxia Mountain

Use one complete line: **Elder Peak direction → viewpoints → Yin Yuan Stone / Xianglong Lake area → return before evening**. Add Yangyuan Mountain only if weather and energy are good; after a tiring course, keep one shorter line.

The rain and cloud outlook for the 25th is a 21 August 2026 research snapshot, not a guarantee. Shorten the route under rain and follow temporary scenic-area controls; stairs and viewpoints can be slippery.

### 26 August: Guangdong Grand Canyon in Ruyuan

The canyon boardwalks, waterfalls, deep valley, forest and streams fit the preferred landscape better, but it is not in the same direction as Danxia Mountain. Book a car or ride in advance, or return to Shaoguan city on the 25th and leave for Ruyuan early on the 26th; do not rely on a last-minute bus connection.

Cancel the descent and valley-bottom route in sustained heavy rain. Waterfalls may be stronger after rain, but stairs, boardwalks and the valley are also more slippery. Follow closures and on-site safety staff.

### 27 August: return to Changsha

Sleep naturally in the morning, then take the high-speed rail from Shaoguan to Changsha. Use 12306 for the live service and availability; third-party fastest-time tables are not guarantees.

## B: Beihai + Weizhou Island

Beihai is a different trip from Guilin and Yangshuo, and the island—not only the city—is the reason to go. But ships, sea conditions and weather make it less robust than Shaoguan.

### Activation conditions

1. Both outbound and return Beihai–Weizhou tickets are confirmed;
2. There is no suspension notice, dangerous sea condition or scenic-area control;
3. Island accommodation remains cancellable; do not buy a non-refundable package first;
4. Keep at least one buffer day for the return;
5. Do not travel to Beihai on the evening the course ends; recovery comes first.

### Reference flow: 25–29 August

- **25 Aug:** if recovery is normal, keep a light 1–1.5 hour tennis session; check out and travel from Humen via Guangzhou South to Beihai;
- **26 Aug:** take the ship to Weizhou only if services are normal; choose Crocodile Mountain or Shiluokou for the afternoon;
- **27 Aug:** Wucaitan, the Catholic church and villages, Shell Beach or the north coast; rest indoors around midday;
- **28 Aug:** return to Beihai, keeping space for ship delays, then choose Silver Beach or the old street;
- **29 Aug:** return from Beihai to Changsha using the live 12306 option.

Do not buy only the outbound island ticket. Do not lock non-refundable island accommodation before the ship plan is confirmed. Wind and rain in the Beibu Gulf are the main failure points.

## Other backups

- **Huizhou Nan'kun Mountain / Shuangyue Bay:** a mountain-and-coast combination without a ferry, but the transfer is large; choose one rather than forcing both;
- **Chaozhou + Nan'ao Island:** old town, coast roads, lighthouses and Chaoshan food; more interruption-resistant than Beihai because there is no ferry;
- **Qingyuan:** if selected, compare Lianzhou Underground River + Liannan Yao villages rather than making Yingxi Peak Forest the first choice;
- **Zhaoqing:** an easy two-day option, but its lake-and-peak imagery may feel less new after Guilin.

## Final selection order

1. Only 2–3 extra days: Shaoguan Danxia Mountain + Guangdong Grand Canyon;
2. If the trip can run to 29 or 30 August and ships are confirmed by the 24th: Beihai + Weizhou Island;
3. If the priority is coast roads, lighthouses and photography: Chaozhou + Nan'ao;
4. If the priority is an easy pace: choose either Nan'kun Mountain or Shuangyue Bay.

Train times, weather, ships and prices on this page are not locked. Recheck 12306, official ferry channels, scenic notices and weather warnings before departure.
