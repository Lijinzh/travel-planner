## If Yangshuo becomes a standalone future trip

The notes below are retained for a separate future trip and should not be mixed into the current Dongguan-to-Shaoguan plan.

## Transfer boundary

- Do not chase the last Yangshuo train on 24 August; complete training, assessment, make-up work and recovery first;
- Play tennis on the morning of 25 August only if your condition is good, then shower, check out, eat and go to Humen station;
- Use 12306 for the final Humen–Yangshuo service and availability; do not treat a third-party fastest-time table as a guarantee;
- On the first night in Yangshuo, check in, eat and rest. No night cycling.

## 26 August: Yulong River and rural cycling

Suggested rhythm:

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 08:00–09:00 | Breakfast, water and sun protection |
| 09:00–12:00 | Yulong River and Jiuxian area |
| 12:00–15:30 | Lunch and a nap away from peak heat |
| 16:00–19:00 | Short bicycle or compliant e-bike line |
| After 19:30 | Dinner and rest |

A candidate line is **Jiuxian Village → Yulong River paths → Jima Pier area → outer Ten-Mile Gallery → return**. Keep the total around 15–25 km and shorten it for thunder, standing water, road conditions or fatigue. Prefer a bicycle or compliant e-bike in Yangshuo; do not improvise with an unregistered motorcycle.

Decide on bamboo rafting and other water activities from the day's water level, operations, weather and cancellation terms. Do not lock in non-refundable activities too early.

## 27 August: Xingping landscape day

Xingping Ancient Town, the RMB 20 view and the Li River waterfront can fill a day. Add a viewpoint only in good weather; in rain or on slippery roads, keep to the town and riverside and skip wet mountain paths.

## 28 August: return

Do not schedule a formal attraction in the morning. Check out early, travel to Yangshuo station and arrive at least 45 minutes before departure. Use 12306 for the same-day direct or connecting option.

## Weather substitutions

- Sun / cloud: cycle in the morning and after 16:00, avoiding noon;
- Light rain: shorten cycling and use villages, cafés or indoor county-town activities;
- Thunderstorms: cancel cycling, riverside exposure and mountain roads and enter a reliable building;
- Heavy rain: stay at the hotel or commercial area and adjust the stay or return if needed.

## How to choose a stay

- Yulong River / Jiuxian: easier for early and late cycling, usually quieter;
- Outside central Yangshuo: easier for food, ride-hailing and departure;
- The core West Street area: potentially noisy at night unless the commercial centre is the point.

## If Yangshuo is dropped

Return after the 24th course or add one recovery night in Dongguan. Do not fill eight days by overpacking the course, tennis and landscape cycling. Keep Xichong and Shuangyue Bay surfing for a separate trip.
