FRIEND DISCUSSION · RESEARCH FILE

Museums and photo locations

First tier

Keyuan Museum

A compact Qing-era Lingnan garden with corridors, windows, water and rockwork that repeatedly frame one another. Soft morning light or rain works well. The current official guide lists 09:00–17:30, Tuesday closure and ¥8 for the historic garden; commercial photography is restricted.

Opium War Museum, Naval Battle Museum and Weiyuan Fort

Dongguan's most important modern-history route. See the exhibition first, then read the Pearl River geography and gun positions on site. Reservations may be separate; a suspension occurred in 2026, so check the official account.

China Agarwood Culture Museum and Xiangshi Park

Liaobu was a historic agarwood market. Official material describes this as China's first agarwood-themed museum. Confirm the current closed day, indoor photography and tower access.

Second tier: photographs and city character

Public Songshan Lake waterfront

Use early or golden-hour light for reflections, paths, bridges and compressed views of distant European-style buildings. Huawei's Ox Horn campus is an office campus and online accounts describe employee hosting for entry; without it, photograph only public views.

Liyuzhou Creative Park

Official water-authority material describes it as an important industrial relic of Dongguan's early rural industrialisation. Brick, steel, old factory forms and water suit blue-hour environmental portraits. Do not enter closed buildings.

Old Guancheng and the Dongguan Memory area

Good for street documentary, arcades and old / new contrasts. Official material describes the first area as including Xicheng Tower Park, the west Shanzhou River section and the Zhongxing Road arcade street; in January 2026, Guancheng confirmed the opening of the second area on Daxi Road and the activation of the east river section. Follow current worksites and public boundaries rather than old route posts. If a training day leaves only a short free window, use the old-town and light-cycling module instead of forcing a full old-city route.

Nanshe Ming–Qing Village

Ancestral halls, ponds, grey sculpture, brick carving and flowers provide strong architectural photographs. Commercial platforms still list tickets, but confirm the official price and hours.

Huangqi / Qifeng Mountain

The red lantern, temple and skyline are locally recognisable. Hike only in cool, storm-free conditions and not when recovering from depth training.

Do not schedule yet

The old Dongguan Museum closed on 4 March 2026. Provincial material suggested an October 2026 trial opening for the new venue, but it is not open until the museum announces it.

Social-media photo ideas are discovery leads, not permission: do not enter roads, tracks, offices, worksites or abandoned buildings; ask before photographing residents and businesses; verify drone airspace and site rules.
