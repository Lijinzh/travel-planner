# Motorcycles, rail and cross-town movement

## Conclusion

**A motorcycle cannot be the default entertainment on this 22–24 August course trip.** Dongguan introduced restricted areas and roads in 2007; later official responses continued to describe area- and time-based controls. In 2026 the city is running a year-long motorcycle / e-bike enforcement campaign, and Songshan Lake has publicised drone, video and road patrols. Fatigue, equalisation and attention after depth training also make riding a poor follow-on activity.

Seeing motorcycles on the road does not mean a visitor can legally rent one and ride anywhere. An official case also records a motorcycle violating a prohibition sign on Binhaiwan Avenue.

## Every condition required before rental

- Correct Chinese motorcycle licence category for the vehicle; C1 / C2 alone is insufficient.
- Valid registration, plate, vehicle licence and insurance.
- Written rental contract with accident, rescue and deposit terms.
- Standard helmet and no unregistered, hidden-plate or illegally modified vehicle.
- Written confirmation of every proposed main road from the rental company and Dongguan traffic police / 12345.
- Dry weather, good visibility and no post-depth fatigue.

If one condition fails, do not rent.

For this trip, treat “I will ride after the course” as off by default. Without written route confirmation, valid vehicle documents and enough recovery time, use walking, ride-hailing or public transport instead.

If what you want is the “bike plus landscape photographs” feeling, use a short bicycle ride on public Songshan Lake waterfront paths instead: a legitimate bicycle, light helmet and waterproof bag, with an 8–12 km out-and-back limit. This is not a default activity after open-water / depth training; cancel for thunder, standing water, strong wind or a lack of instructor approval. See the [optional module page](../activities/optional-walk-bike.md) for scheduling.

## Routes not to plan

- Casual riding through the central urban districts;
- An unverified full Songshan Lake circuit;
- Binhaiwan Avenue or other signed restricted roads;
- Intercity expressways towards Humen, Shenzhen or Huizhou;
- Night mountain rides, rain or alcohol;
- Office campuses, construction roads, greenways or pedestrian boardwalks.

## Practical transport mix

| Trip | Preferred mode | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Changsha–Dongguan | High-speed rail | Lock on 12306 after dates are known |
| Station–Songshan Lake | Ride-hailing / instructor transfer | Easier with long fins |
| Local Songshan Lake | Walk, legal bicycle, ride-hailing | Better for photography stops |
| Songshan Lake–Guancheng | Ride-hailing or rail plus ride-hailing | Allow cross-town time |
| Guancheng–Humen | Metro Line 2 direction plus transfer / ride-hailing | Use live mapping |
| Dongguan–Shenzhen / Huizhou | Rail, licensed car or rental car | Better for surf equipment |

If legal riding is ultimately confirmed, limit it to a two- to four-hour daylight loop returning to the same licensed shop, with full protective gear and no sightseeing deadline. Specific roads are intentionally not published until rechecked.
