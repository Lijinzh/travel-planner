## My competition baseline

- Men's division;
- AIDA 2-Star, 3-Star and 4-Star freediver certifications obtained;
- Personal recorded Static Apnea (STA) best: **4:04**;
- No official competition history, active AIDA national-membership status, competition medical signed within one year, or DYN / DYNB personal best has been supplied yet.

**Conclusion:** the certifications and STA ability are sufficient for ordinary AIDA open pool competitions. A 4:04 is not an open-division podium result, but it is a valid starting level for a first competition. Final eligibility still depends on age, membership, medical documents, accepted registration and each event's supplementary rules.

### PRC rookie-team match

The current PRC notice defines rookie-team athletes as competitors who have never entered a freediving competition and obtained an AIDA 2-equivalent certification after 1 July 2025, or do not yet hold an equivalent certification. The recorded August 2026 certifications satisfy the timing condition; if there is no previous official competition, ask the organiser to confirm rookie eligibility. PRC is team-entry only rather than ordinary individual registration.

## Historical rank reference for 4:04

- 2026 Greater Bay Area first-stop men's STA: 4:06 ranked 11th and 3:49 ranked 12th; adding a valid 4:04 would place it at roughly **12th of 15 scoring results**;
- 2025 first-stop men's STA: 4:07 ranked 20th and 4:02 ranked 21st; a 4:04 would place at roughly **21st of 30 scoring results**;
- A 4:04 therefore sits around the lower-middle / final third of a Greater-Bay-scale men's open field, and may sit closer to mid-field in a smaller local competition.

This is a cross-event estimate only. A training PB becomes an official result only after check-in, Official Top, required touches, the surface protocol and a valid judging card.

## Documents and preparation to complete

- [ ] Log into AIDA and confirm profile, nationality and active national-member status;
- [ ] Prepare the event's medical form signed by a physician within one year;
- [ ] Prepare photo identification / passport and liability forms;
- [ ] Ask whether the event requires active AIDA membership, local-association membership or extra sports insurance;
- [ ] Confirm disciplines, fee, refund rules, AP deadline, pool familiarisation and briefing;
- [ ] Practise the official surface protocol; do not automatically use the training PB as the announced performance;
- [ ] Record a valid 25-metre or 50-metre-pool DYN / DYNB best before estimating dynamic-event ranking;
- [ ] Always train STA, DYN, DYNB and DNF with qualified safety cover / a buddy; never practise breath-hold alone.

## Extra barrier for national-federation events

The 2026 CSUPF registration notice says athletes should normally belong to a group member. The regular registration period was 1 January to 31 March, with supplementary registration possible by advance application in special circumstances. Before entering the national championship or Golden League, ask a registered club about the representation agreement, supplementary paperwork, insurance and entry channel rather than relying on AIDA certification alone.
