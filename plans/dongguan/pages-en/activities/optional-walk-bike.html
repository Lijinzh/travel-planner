Optional modules: light Dongguan activities and cross-city alternatives

Checked 21 August 2026. This page turns the latest entertainment ideas into conditional modules, not confirmed bookings. Freediving, equalisation, sleep and recovery always come first.

Scheduling boundary

  • 22–24 August: keep course days free of a formal old-town walk or ride; only take a very short waterfront walk if the instructor approves, there is no depth session and weather is stable.
  • 25 August in the default Shaoguan plan: Danxia Mountain is the main task; do not schedule tennis. Only in the Beihai branch, with normal recovery, keep 1–1.5 hours of light tennis.
  • 26 August: the Grand Canyon is a weather-sensitive mountain branch; do not use a Dongguan ride as a substitute for a safety decision. Cancel the descent in heavy rain.
  • 27 August: return to Changsha from Shaoguan by default; the Beihai branch needs ship-delay margin.

The attached proposal's “10:00–18:00 theory / confined water” and “10:00–13:00 open water” blocks are planning examples only. Do not publish them as a fixed timetable until the venue or instructor confirms them in writing.

A | Guancheng old-town night walk

The first Dongguan Memory opening area includes Xicheng Tower Park, the west section of Shanzhou River and the Zhongxing Road arcades. An official Guancheng update in January 2026 confirmed that the second area on Daxi Road had opened and that the east section of Shanzhou River had been restored. It works as a relaxed city-observation walk, not as a full sightseeing circuit forced into a training day.

Suggested line

Zhongxing Road arcades / Xicheng Tower Park → Daxi Road → the Shanzhou River waterfront → a nearby dessert or late meal.

Keep it to 60–90 minutes and follow current public boundaries, construction barriers and crowd conditions. This is a living neighbourhood: do not cross barriers, enter closed buildings or stand in traffic lanes for photographs.

When to cancel

Cancel for a late finish, thunderstorm, equalisation discomfort, dizziness or obvious fatigue. No alcohol on 22–24 August, and do not turn an optional walk into a late night.

B | Light Songshan Lake cycling instead of a motorcycle

In 2026, Dongguan's ecology bureau described Songshan Lake as having a connected greenway network and about 35 km of waterfront paths around Songmushan Reservoir. That supports a light ride on public waterfront paths, but it does not mean every campus, road or shoreline is open for unrestricted cycling.

Conservative version

  • Use a legitimate bicycle or shared bike, with a light cycling helmet, dry-fit clothes and a waterproof bag;
  • Start near a public Songhu Misty Rain entrance, follow public waterfront paths, take photographs and turn back;
  • Target 8–12 km, not the full lake circuit;
  • Light after 16:30 is usually better for photographs, but cancel for thunderstorm warnings, standing water or poor wind conditions;
  • At the first sound of thunder, leave the waterfront, open lawns and trees for a reliable building.

Huawei's office campus and European-style buildings are distant public-view subjects only. Online “European town” routes are not proof of visitor access.

C | Rain fallback

  • Keyuan Museum: check the official website's same-day opening and reservation status, then use it for an indoor garden / architecture session. Do not treat an old opening-hours screenshot as a guarantee.
  • Guancheng old town: for a brief shower, shorten the walk to arcades and food areas with cover; cancel in sustained thunderstorm conditions.
  • Tennis: only contact an indoor Songshan Lake court when the instructor confirms there is no depth training and your recovery is good. Use the tennis venue and booking strategy.

D | Order after the Dongguan course

If the course finishes safely on the 24th and recovery is adequate, eat, rest and move to Shaoguan by default; visit Danxia Mountain on the 25th and reassess the Grand Canyon on the 26th. Only if both Beihai–Weizhou ship legs are confirmed before the 24th should light tennis and a Beihai transfer be considered. If training runs late, add a night or return; do not stack multiple cross-city activities into one recovery window.

Sources and limits

  • Dongguan Memory's Daxi Road opening: official Guancheng information on the second opening area and the east Shanzhou River section.
  • Dongguan Memory opening background: government material describing the first area's Xicheng Tower Park, Shanzhou River and arcade-block scope.
  • Songshan Lake's green-city profile: Dongguan ecology-bureau background on greenways, waterfront paths and public ecological space; it does not guarantee same-day bike stock or route permission.
